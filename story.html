Excalibur Line: A Conversation Starter

A magnetic hover train, Fort Valley first — and a line to Las Vegas that Vegas gets to name

Omni-Present Solutions, Inc.
Honorable Jay Ladon Dickey Sr. EL, CEO and Founder

Every big build starts as an argument somebody is willing to have in public. Here's ours.

The Excalibur Line is Omni-Present Solutions' overhead magnetic hover train: the train hangs below a single overhead rail, held by magnetic force with a visible gap — zero friction, no wheels, no contact, no ground track — gliding high over the town below. Jay's stated design The route starts where the company starts: Fort Valley, Georgia — town, to school, to factory, to a Sky Hub in the sky, with the train running into the Sky Hub. The train carries exactly two lines of lettering: "Excalibur Line" and "Fort Valley Georgia."

Fort Valley is first on purpose. The factory — 800,000 square feet, with the Rail Academy at the same Fort Valley site Jay's announced plan — is planned to break ground in March 2027, with the school training the people who build and run the line, and scholarships committed yearly for Georgia youth and young adults to become certified in skilled labor positions with job placement opportunities. The announced Phase 1 is a $450 million program with 3,500+ jobs Jay's announced figures, from the company's October 27, 2026 release.

The speed claim — stated the way engineers state it

Jay's stated design target Top speed up to Mach 1 — as a design target. And here is the qualifier that travels with it everywhere, because a number without its conditions is how bad records start: the speed will depend on the amount of stops between destinations, the distance between each stop, and the turns required and inclines over terrains; speed is determined by frequency and timing studies, within the limits permitted by regulations. Anyone quoting the Mach 1 figure without the qualifier is misquoting the project.

The road to Vegas — and who gets to name it

The Line's announced direction runs from Fort Valley through its hub states toward Las Vegas — and here's the part of the story that's genuinely open: the line that connects to the Excalibur Line in Las Vegas has no name yet, because the Vegas partners will name it, with the name to be determined by 2027, before groundbreaking. Jay's stated plan

The people backing that connection have a name of their own: Founding Supporters — and the title means exactly one thing: they are founders of connecting the Excalibur Line to Las Vegas. Not owners of the company, not shareholders of the Line — founders of the connection. That distinction is printed wherever the tiers appear, because this project corrects its record before anyone else has to.
