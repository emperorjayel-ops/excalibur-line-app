About this app

Excalibur Line is the official app of the Excalibur Line top-rail magnetic hover train project — Fort Valley, Georgia to the Nine states, with a projected 2027 launch. The app presents the project, memberships, supporter tiers, education and scholarship information, and links to the other Omni-Present Solutions apps. The app is a preview experience: accounts and requests created in the app are session-only.

What data the app handles

  • Session-only accounts and forms: information you type into sign-up, membership, or request forms is held only in the app session on your device and is cleared when the session/page closes. It is not transmitted to or stored on our servers by this app.
  • No analytics or tracking: this app does not include third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • External links: the app links to outside websites (for example social media and press sites). Those sites have their own privacy policies and are not covered by this one.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks or ad trackers in this app.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, call logs, or device identifiers for advertising.

Payments and financial features

All payment features shown in this app — including Bitcoin membership requests and Black Card load requests — are demo/preview only. The app builds a demo payment request with a request code; no Bitcoin address is shown, no real payment is taken, and no money moves through this app.

Data retention

Because app accounts and requests are session-only, there is no account database from this app to retain or delete. If you email us, we keep that correspondence as long as needed to answer you.

Children

This app is directed to users 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date before the changes take effect in the app.
